# SALDY1209 L.L.C. drone website

## Visual direction

A diagonal from a quadcopter's airframe becomes the cut in the hero image, the angle of the logo, and the waypoints in the service index. A large aircraft in high-desert daylight opens the page against a low-contrast aerial background of field parcels at blue hour. Visitors choose their mission from an equipment-to-field sequence and take the selection into the email draft. The narrow layout keeps the same order and gives the aircraft its own crop.

Taste Skill's `one-motif` direction guided the airframe chamfer and clean photo-led hierarchy. The existing local Work Sans family replaces the suggested remote type. The aviation image palette gives the signal accent its orange tone, and the navy hero makes space for the desert photography. Impeccable informed the contact states, information hierarchy and device-specific composition. The local SpaceX, IBM and Tesla references informed the treatment of equipment at scale, clear operational reading order and restrained navigation. No reference assets are used. The previous poppy masthead and serif layout were retired.

## Content and imagery

The homepage introduces the four confirmed business areas, then shows an equipment storage and staging spread. Service detail pages describe the information needed for an inquiry. The applications gallery presents twelve distinct examples and identifies the scenes as illustrative. Company facts, city map and contact details are retained. The inquiry form opens a draft for review in the visitor's email application.

The aircraft and field scenes were generated for this project. They carry no manufacturer logos and are used as illustrative examples. The twelve gallery images, homepage hero, and aerial background are stored as compressed local WebP. The JPEG social image is a crop of the hero for link previews. See [asset notes](docs/DRONE-IMAGERY.md) for the individual scenes. The font license is in `public/fonts/`.

No binary skill engine, hook or automated browser workaround is used. Visual inspection uses the permitted in-app browser.
